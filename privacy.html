Overview

CODEX Business Operations ("CODEX BizOps," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at codexbizops.com (the "Site"). We provide professional website design, hosting, and management services for small business owners across the United States.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our Site or submit a form, how we use and protect that information, and what choices you have. By using this Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Plain English Summary: We collect the information you give us through our forms, use analytics and advertising tools to understand how people find us, and only contact you with marketing if you opt in. We don't sell your data. Ever.

Section 4

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies on our Site. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us recognize you and understand how you interact with our content.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies — Required for the Site to function properly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — We use Google Analytics to understand visitor behavior, traffic sources, and usage patterns. This data is aggregated and anonymized where possible. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Advertising cookies — We may use cookies from Google Ads and Facebook (Meta) to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns and to show relevant ads to people who have visited our Site. These platforms may combine this data with other information they have collected about you.
  • CRM & form tracking cookies — Our forms are powered by GoHighLevel (GHL), which may use its own cookies to track form interactions and attribute leads.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse new cookies, delete existing cookies, or be notified when a cookie is set. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our Site.

For advertising opt-outs, you can also visit optout.aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

Section 6

Marketing Communications

Email Marketing

We may send you promotional emails about our services, special offers, or relevant updates — but only if you have explicitly opted in to receive them. Every marketing email we send will include a clear and easy way to unsubscribe. Opting out will not affect your ability to receive transactional messages related to an active service agreement.

SMS / Text Message Marketing

We may send promotional text messages to your mobile number if you have provided express written consent to receive SMS marketing from CODEX BizOps. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency will vary. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. For help, reply HELP or contact us at our contact form.

Transactional Communications

Regardless of your marketing preferences, we may contact you with messages that are necessary to fulfill our services — such as responding to your inquiry, delivering your mock-up, or communicating about your active account. These are not marketing messages and are not subject to opt-out.

Age Requirements

Our services are intended for individuals who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18 without verified parental or guardian involvement.

If a minor under the age of 18 wishes to use our services, a parent or legal guardian must be the account holder, must complete all forms and agreements on the minor's behalf, and must be the primary point of contact for all business communications.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor without appropriate parental consent, please contact us immediately at our contact form and we will take prompt steps to remove that information.
